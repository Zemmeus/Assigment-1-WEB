Why students choose EduSphere

Three things make the difference between watching a tutorial and actually learning a skill.

Live classes twice a week

Lessons run in real time, so you can interrupt the instructor and ask questions. Every session is recorded for later revision.

A mentor for every student

Your mentor reviews homework line by line and holds a one-to-one call each week to plan what you study next.

Verified certificate

Finish the final project, defend it in front of the group and receive a certificate with a public verification link.

How learning works

Five steps from sign-up to certificate

  1. Create a free account and take the short placement test.
  2. Choose a course and a study format — evening, weekend or intensive.
  3. Attend live classes and submit the weekly practice task.
  4. Get written feedback from your mentor within 24 hours.
  5. Build and defend a final project, then collect your certificate.

What is included

Everything comes with the subscription

  • Lifetime access to recorded lessons and lecture notes
  • Weekly code review from a working developer
  • Private student chat with instructors and alumni
  • Interview preparation workshops every month
  • Free retake of any module during the same year
