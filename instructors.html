Meet the instructors

Every course is led by a practising developer or designer who still works in the industry. Classes are small on purpose: one instructor never takes more than eighteen students per group.

Marat Zhaksybek

Front-end · HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Seven years of commercial front-end work, currently a senior developer in a fintech company. Teaches the foundations course the way it should be taught — markup first, decoration after.

Specialisations

  • Semantic HTML and accessibility
  • Modern CSS layout: flexbox and grid
  • Vanilla JavaScript and the DOM

Qualifications

  1. BSc in Computer Science, Astana IT University
  2. Senior Front-End Developer since 2022
  3. Speaker at two regional web conferences

Aigerim Baitursyn

Specialisations

  • Dashboard design and data storytelling

Qualifications

Nurlan Serik

Design · UX research, UI systems

Product designer in an e-commerce team. Runs the design course around one rule: every screen a student draws must be justified by something a real user said.

How code review works

  1. Push your homework to your own GitHub repository.
  2. Post the repository link in the group chat thread.
  3. A mentor answers with line-by-line comments within 24 hours.
  4. Fix the comments and mark the task as done.

Want to teach with us?

We are always looking for practising developers who enjoy explaining things. Send a short note about your experience through the contact form and choose the topic “Becoming an instructor”.

  • At least 3 years of industry experience
  • 4–6 free hours per week
  • Ready to review student code in writing
